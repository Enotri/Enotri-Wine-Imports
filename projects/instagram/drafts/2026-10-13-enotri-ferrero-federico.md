## Caption
Mango, Piedmont. The Ferrero family has farmed these hills since 1905.

The estate sits on steep, south-facing slopes where the Langhe meets Monferrato: marl and limestone, at altitude. Four generations on, Federico Ferrero leads the winery.

The vines are worked by hand, with cover crops between the rows and low yields. Nebbiolo is the heart of the estate, alongside Barbera, Dolcetto and the whites of the region.

Nvie is the estate's Langhe Nebbiolo. Bright cherry, rose petal, fine tannins. Built for the table.

Trade buyers: request the price list. Link in bio.
