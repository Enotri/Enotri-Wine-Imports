# Enotri compliance check (Alberta liquor advertising)

Enotri is an AGLC-registered liquor agency, so every Instagram post, caption, story, and Reel it publishes counts as liquor advertising.

**Source:** AGLC *Liquor Agency Handbook*, edition dated 2026-09-10, Section 4 (Advertising) and Section 5 (Product promotions):
https://aglc.ca/documents/liquor-agency-handbook. Section 4.1.2 also requires ads to follow the CRTC Code for Broadcast Advertising of Alcoholic Beverages.

This is a working summary for drafting, not legal advice. If a post sits close to a line, flag it for Rawlings rather than deciding it yourself. If the handbook has a newer edition, re-check the sections cited here.

## Run on every Enotri post

Report each line as ✅, or as ⚠️ with the fix.

1. **Accurate and verifiable (4.1.3a).** Every fact traces to `portfolio.ts` or to Rawlings. No invented awards, scores, vintages, vine ages, generation counts, or "since" dates.
2. **Good taste (4.1.3b).** Nothing offensive to the general public.
3. **Doesn't encourage non-drinkers to drink (4.1.4a).** No "even if you don't like wine, you'll love this."
4. **No irresponsible, heavy, or prolonged drinking (4.1.4b–c).** No "bottomless," "drink up," "open another," "wine o'clock," or "mommy juice" humour. No tables crowded with open bottles for a few people.
5. **No health claims (4.1.4d).** No antioxidants, "good for your heart," "low sulphites so no headache," or "clean wine." Organic and biodynamic are farming facts and fine to state. Just don't suggest they make the wine healthier.
6. **No putting down others (4.1.4e).** No swipes like "unlike mass-produced supermarket wine…". Make the contrast with a positive fact about the grower instead.
7. **No minors, nothing aimed at minors (4.1.5).** No one under 18 or who could reasonably be mistaken for a minor. House rule: everyone shown clearly looks 25 or older. No cartoons, fairy tales, nursery rhymes, children's songs, or child-oriented trending audio.
   - Producer family and harvest photos often include children. A photo that's fine on the producer's own site can't run in Enotri's advertising. Crop only if no part of the child remains. Otherwise choose another image.
8. **Drinking scenes (4.2.1).**
   - The situation shown must be legal.
   - A social scene shows no more than **one drink per person**.
   - No alcohol before or while operating any vehicle (motorized or not, bikes included), boating, or doing anything that needs care. Wine appears only after the activity has clearly ended.
9. **Endorsements and success (4.1.6 and CRTC Code).** If a chef, sommelier, or known personality appears, don't imply wine contributed to their success. More broadly, never link wine to social, professional, romantic, or athletic success.
10. **Restaurants, shops, and hotels are licensees (4.1.8–4.1.10).**
    - Enotri can't pay any part of a licensee's advertising, directly or indirectly. A licensee can't receive money or any other benefit from Enotri for advertising Enotri's brands.
    - An organic post on Enotri's own account saying where its wines are poured or sold is Enotri advertising itself, and that's fine once Rawlings confirms the facts.
    - ⚠️ Flag for Rawlings, don't decide: any paid boost of a post whose main message is a licensee's event or venue, any arrangement where a venue is compensated for posting about Enotri wines, and any "co-hosted" promotion where costs are shared.
11. **Contests and giveaways (4.3.12, 4.3.13, 5.8.1, 5.8.2).**
    - Entrants must be 18+ (Alberta's legal drinking age), and entry can never depend on buying or drinking.
    - No free liquor as a sponsorship.
    - On-premise draws run by a licensee must follow 5.8.1: adults only, closing date posted, draw held on-premise at the advertised time.
    - Keep records for 2 years for any prize worth over $100 wholesale.
    - Instagram's promotion rules also need official rules and a statement that Instagram isn't involved.
    - Always mark a giveaway post ⚠️ for Rawlings to approve before it goes live.
12. **Prices (3.8.1).** The wholesale price must be the same for every licensee at any one time. Don't post prices, discounts, or "deals." Point to the price list (trade) or "ask your local shop" (consumers).
13. **Paid creators.** A creator posting for Enotri is a third party acting on Enotri's behalf (4.1.2). Their post has to pass this same checklist and use Instagram's *Paid partnership* label.

## One-time account setup (not checked per post)

- Set the Instagram account's minimum age to 18+ for Canada (in the professional account settings). Meta's branded content policies require alcohol content to be age-restricted.
- Keep the bio factual: what Enotri does, where (Alberta), and a link to the price-list request.
