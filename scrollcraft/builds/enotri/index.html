Intro

Growers, not brands.

Enotri is an independent wine agency built around a simple belief: the best bottles begin with someone who is devoted to a particular piece of land.

We work directly with producers whose dedication comes before commercial branding, then bring those stories to Alberta restaurants, wine shops, hotels, and curious tables. Through the AGLC framework and Liquor Connect, we make the distance between an honest grower and your glass feel shorter.

Questions

Good bottles come with good answers.

How do I get access to your price list?

Send us a note with your business and what you are looking for. We will share the current trade list and the right next step.

Do you work with restaurants and independents?

Yes. We work with restaurants, wine shops, hotels, chefs, and people building thoughtful lists in Alberta.

How are your growers selected?

We look for producers with a real connection to their land, a clear point of view, and wines that say something specific about where they come from.

Can Enotri help with a wine dinner?

Yes. We collaborate with local chefs and accounts on wine dinners, presentations, and tastings that fit the people around the table.
