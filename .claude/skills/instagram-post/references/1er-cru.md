# 1er Cru Elite Services: Instagram voice and content

**The business:** a founder-run cleaning company in Edmonton. Services: post-construction cleaning, re-cleans, possession cleaning, and bi-weekly cleaning for builders' spec homes.

**Audience:** builders, site supers, and project managers who need a house turned over on schedule. Realtors and new owners who need it spotless on possession day.

## Voice: short, precise, cooperative, factually clear

Write like a reliable trade partner reporting a finished job. Give the scope and the result, then stop.

- Line 1 states the job and the result. *"Final clean, two-storey show home. Ready for walkthrough."*
- The caption runs 30–90 words. Builders read on a phone between site visits.
- **Cooperative:** you're part of the build team. *"So your trades can wrap up and your buyers walk into a finished house."*
- Specifics beat superlatives. *"Every vent cover off and washed. Window tracks vacuumed. Drywall dust wiped from every ledge."*
- Use one functional emoji (✓ for a checklist) at most, or none.
- **No claims you can't back up:** "best in Edmonton," "#1," "100% dust-free," "guaranteed." Canada's Competition Act prohibits misleading claims, and builders don't believe them anyway.
- Testimonials: use only real quotes Rawlings provides, with the person's OK to share.

| Write like this | Not like this |
|---|---|
| Possession clean, 3-bed bungalow. Inside cabinets, appliances, baseboards, windows. Keys handed over on time. | We're the BEST cleaners in YEG!!! Your home will SPARKLE ✨✨✨ |
| Post-construction clean starts after the last trade leaves. We work to your walkthrough date. | Don't let dirty houses ruin your sale! |

## Privacy and permission checklist (every photo, every caption)

- ✅ No house numbers, street signs, licence plates, lockbox or alarm codes, mail, documents, or family photos in frame.
- ✅ No faces of clients, homeowners, or trades without their consent.
- ✅ No builder, client, or address named or tagged without written permission. That includes the builder behind the bi-weekly spec-home contract. Describe the work generically ("a spec home in southwest Edmonton") unless Rawlings confirms permission.
- ✅ No visible safety problems on an active site, such as people without PPE where it's required, or blocked exits.
- ✅ Before and after use the same angle and framing, with no misleading edits. Light correction is fine. Removing defects is not.
- ✅ Every claim is checkable (scope, timing, square footage from Rawlings).
