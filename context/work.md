# Business and Work

## Enotri Wine Imports Inc

Enotri imports wines from around the world and markets them into Alberta through AGLC and the Liquor Connect warehouse. The business markets to restaurants, wine shops, and hotels, and organizes wine events and collaborations with local chefs for wine dinners.

Recurring work includes sales calls, scheduling wine presentation appointments, presenting wines, writing follow-up emails, finding new suppliers, and handling AGLC and Liquor Connect requirements.

## 1er Cru Elite Services
